Our Vision & Divine Mandate

We are called and commissioned by God to stand faithfully as spiritual watchmen—sounding the trumpet of truth, holiness, and salvation to every soul and nation.

Isaiah 49:5–6: Appointed to bring back the people unto God and to serve as a light to the nations, that salvation may reach unto the end of the earth.

Ezekiel 33:1–7: Set as a watchman unto the house of God to hear the word at His mouth and give faithful warning from Him.

About Watchman Catholic Charismatic Renewal Movement

The Watchman Catholic Charismatic Renewal Movement is a Christ-centered, Bible-believing ministry dedicated to sound apostolic doctrine, genuine repentance, spiritual awakening, and practical Christian living.

Across dioceses and fellowship centers nationwide, believers gather to worship God in spirit and in truth, grow in the knowledge of His Word, and prepare for the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. Use the GPS locator or browse the alphabetical state directory below to connect with a parish near you.
